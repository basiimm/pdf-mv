A few quick answers

Is it free?
Yes. PDF.mv is free to use and does not require an account.
Are my files uploaded?
No. Supported tools process files locally in your browser — your documents don’t leave your device.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes, on modern mobile and desktop browsers.
Does it work offline?
Once a tool has loaded, most PDF processing keeps working without a connection. Loading the app for the first time still needs one.
